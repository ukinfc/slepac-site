Questions

Why can't I see this month's total?
That's the point. The current month stays hidden until it's over, so you log honestly instead of watching a number. On the 1st, last month is revealed in History, Calendar and Categories.

I really need to peek.
On the Log tab, tap the sealed card that says "No peeking until…". The month unlocks. Tap it again to hide it.

What does "6k" or "12k" mean?
Shorthand item names carry their own price, so "6k" logs as 6,000 without typing a price.

How are EUR and USD converted?
At the day's rate when you log the purchase. The rate is saved with the purchase, so old months never change. Rates by Exchange Rate API.

How do I log from the Lock Screen or Action button?
Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add the Slepac widget. Control Center or Action button: add the "Log a purchase" control in Control Center or in Settings › Action Button.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?
Settings › Backup › choose a folder (iCloud Drive is best). Slepac saves a fresh backup once a day when you open the app. On the new phone, go to Settings › Backup › Restore from backup and pick the file.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone, and in your backup folder if you set one. See the Privacy Policy.
