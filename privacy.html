Privacy Policy

Slepac · Effective October 22, 2026

Slepac is a spending log that keeps your data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your information.

What the app stores

The purchases you log (item name, price, currency, date, and where you made them if Save location is on), your categories, your recurring payments and your settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They never leave your device unless you set up a backup yourself.

Location

If you allow it, Slepac saves where you were when you log a purchase so it can show that place on a map. The app only asks for access while you're using it, and you can turn this off at any time with Save location in Settings or in your iPhone's Settings. Locations are stored with your purchases on your device and in any backups you set up. They are never sent to the developer. To show a short address, Slepac uses Apple's location services, and Apple's privacy policy applies.

Backups

If you turn on backups, Slepac writes a JSON file to a folder you choose in the Files app. If that folder is in iCloud Drive, the file is stored by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's privacy policy applies. The developer has no access to it. You can turn off backups or delete the file at any time.

Exchange rates

To convert EUR and USD to dinars, Slepac downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) at most once a day. The request contains no personal information and none of your purchases. As with any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address. See ExchangeRate-API's terms.

Widgets and controls

The Lock Screen widget and the Control Center control only open the app. They don't read or show any of your data.

Children

Slepac doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual purchases in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Any backup files you created stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
